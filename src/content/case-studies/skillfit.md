solution:
  title: We helped Skillfit.io build the outbound system themselves.
  paragraphs:
    - Skillfit.io did not want an agency to simply hand over booked meetings. Outbound was a core part of the company's value creation, so Lukas wanted the team to understand the system and stay close to how it worked.
    - "Enablement helped Skillfit.io set up the foundations: secondary domains, email infrastructure, the outbound toolset, Clay templates, bilingual copywriting support, and a structured process for building and testing campaigns."
    - The work also forced strategic clarity. Skillfit.io used the setup process to think more deeply about its value proposition, buyer personas, use cases, trigger events, and the segments most likely to respond.
solutionSteps:
  - title: Clarify the ICP and trigger events
    body: Move beyond broad workplace learning messaging and identify more specific buying moments, such as regulatory changes, compliance requirements, and industry-specific training needs.
  - title: Build the outbound infrastructure
    body: Set up secondary domains, email infrastructure, outbound tooling, and workflows that could support higher volume without relying on the primary domain.
  - title: Use templates instead of starting from scratch
    body: Give Skillfit.io access to Clay templates, AI prompts, worksheets, and frameworks so the team could build faster while still understanding how the system worked.
  - title: Coach the team through implementation
    body: Use group calls, Slack support, peer learning, and account feedback to help the team get unstuck, improve campaigns, and keep ownership of the motion.
