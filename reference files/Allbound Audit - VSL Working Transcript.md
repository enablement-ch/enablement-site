## 04:42-05:08 - Principle 1: Build the Business Case

Most sellers explain features without determining what the customer's problem costs. If neither the seller nor the customer can quantify the problem, there is no urgency. Without urgency, there is no decision, and the offer stays in the nice-to-have category.

**Function:** Show why activity with the wrong stakeholder is not progress.

### Opening Diagnosis

Your pipeline problem is probably not a lack of activity. It is the places where intent, context, and opportunities disappear between systems.

### Promise

1. Correct signals and account prioritization
