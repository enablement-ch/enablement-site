## Audit details

- Completely free
- Plan for 30 minutes, with up to 45 minutes if needed
- Lanny runs the audit
- Prospect provides current funnel and commercial numbers
- CRM or dashboard sharing is optional but improves precision
- Every participant receives a written diagnosis within 24 hours
- Diagnosis includes bottlenecks, evidence, priority order, and what can be fixed internally
- If Enablement can solve the problem, the next step can move into scoping
- Public copy should not discuss qualification rates, rejected companies, or describe the call as a disguised qualification call

## ICP used on the page

- B2B SaaS and tech-enabled service companies
- Product-market fit required
- Usually $1M to $15M in annual revenue
- Minimum roughly $500K in annual revenue
- Strongest fit: no repeatable outbound system or a broken/incomplete one
- Not for pre-product-market-fit companies
