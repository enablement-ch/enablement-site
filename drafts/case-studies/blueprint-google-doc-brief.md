## Customer context

Blueprint is a health and longevity company building an international distribution network. Dario Hong, Managing Director International, was responsible for expanding that network across 30 countries within six months without relying on a large global BD team.

## The challenge

Blueprint already had access to Claude, Codex, Sales Navigator, Apollo, and other prospecting tools. The team tried to build the motion internally, using AI for company research and manually assembling country-specific lists.

That approach could identify individual companies and support one market at a time. It could not create simultaneous coverage across markets such as Japan, Chile, Germany, and China. In several regions, particularly Asian markets, the relevant company and contact data was fragmented or missing from common databases.

The limitation was not access to AI. Blueprint needed experts who could turn those tools into a precise, repeatable international outbound system.

> "We had already tried to, I would say, the obvious things, is like ourselves using tools like Claude, Sales Navigator, Apollo, and so on. And I would say, to be honest, that worked to a certain extent, but it really didn't scale."

## The solution

Enablement translated Blueprint's international expansion strategy into a complete outbound operating system.

The work included:

- Market and ICP research
- Account identification and list building
- Data enrichment and buyer validation
- AI-assisted prospecting workflows
- Email infrastructure and campaign execution
- Messaging and campaign management
- LinkedIn profile development
- LinkedIn content and automated outreach
- Reporting, testing, and ongoing optimization

Instead of asking AI to produce generic company lists, the system used market-specific criteria and multiple data sources to identify the right distributors, clinics, companies, and decision-makers.

LinkedIn became a second acquisition channel. Enablement helped build Dario's presence from scratch, shape the initial content and messaging, and operate outreach that generated personal conversations and meetings alongside email.

## What we did

### 1. Defined each market and buyer

Translated the global distribution mandate into specific account, market, and decision-maker criteria for each country.

### 2. Built the research and data system

Combined AI, databases, enrichment, market-specific sources, and human validation to find relevant companies that generic searches missed.

### 3. Launched email and LinkedIn outreach

Converted qualified data into coordinated campaigns, personal conversations, and booked meetings across both channels.

### 4. Operated and optimized the engine

Managed campaign execution, reporting, messaging tests, and targeting improvements so the motion became more precise as it scaled.

## The results

Within six months, the system generated an average of 30-40 qualified meetings per month.

Dario estimated that approximately 95% of his calls were with the intended buyer personas and decision-makers inside the right companies.

The motion helped Blueprint establish distribution coverage across 30 countries without a dedicated global BD team. It generated a high eight-figure qualified pipeline and contributed to more than $10M in closed revenue.

LinkedIn also became a repeatable meeting channel, despite Dario starting with no previous experience using it for business development.

## Outcome

Blueprint went from using AI for individual searches to operating an expert-built global outbound system. Manual research and one-off lists were replaced by a coordinated motion that delivered qualified conversations directly to Dario's calendar.

The critical difference was not the tools. It was the targeting logic, data workflows, validation, messaging, campaign execution, and optimization wrapped around them.

## Final lesson

Having Claude and Codex is not the same as having an outbound system. AI accelerates the work, but experts turn it into qualified pipeline and revenue.
