# Editorial note: How to turn LinkedIn content into qualified sales meetings

Drafted and approved for publication 2026-11-02. Public article: `src/content/blog/how-to-turn-linkedin-content-into-qualified-sales-meetings.md`. It is marked `draft: false`.

## Original perspective and decisions

- Gilbert's 2026-11-02 voice note supplies the central diagnosis: content and sales run separately, content is judged by impressions and likes, and engagement has no reliable path into pipeline.
- The process described is his: create buyer-relevant content and useful assets; observe available engagement; qualify companies and contacts; start contextual outreach; classify replies with AI; route positive hand raisers to sales with context.
- Engagement strength is described as a practical judgment, not a measured conversion ranking. Likes and profile visits alone are not treated as buyer intent.
- Website visitors are not assumed to be identifiable individuals. Profile-view visibility depends on the viewer's privacy setting.
- "Monitor all engagement" was narrowed to engagement the team can actually observe and use. The article does not recommend scraping LinkedIn or automating actions through prohibited tools.
- The example outreach message is illustrative, not a customer quote or a measured result.
- The Ekipa/Caritas example is supported by the current public case study. The broader 15-30 meetings per week result is deliberately not attributed to LinkedIn content alone.
