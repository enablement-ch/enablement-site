# Editorial note: What is content engineering?

Drafted and approved for publication 2026-11-02. Public article: `src/content/blog/what-is-content-engineering.md`. It is marked `draft: false`.

## Angle and source decisions

- The user supplied [Slate's definition and framework](https://slatehq.com/blog/content-engineering) and [Gallium's content engineer article](https://gallium.ai/blog/rise-of-the-content-engineer) for research. Both describe systems behind content, including source material, structure, production, channel adaptation, and feedback. Neither article was copied or cited in the public draft.
- The draft defines the term broadly, then focuses on a B2B GTM application that fits Enablement: connecting founder expertise, proof, content production, audience response, and sales action.
- Gilbert's 2026-11-02 follow-up supplies Enablement's operating process: client and prospect interviews, a voice file, approved posts as structure and tone references, first graphics made manually and retained as design references, skills for research/ideation/structure/publishing, and strategist plus human writer review. The revised article uses this as original company material.
- Gilbert clarified the closing offer: Enablement sells done-for-you content plus the outbound conversion mechanism that acts on qualified content engagement. The closing paragraph and CTA reflect this.
- "Approved posts as references" does not imply that a model was fine-tuned on client data. The workflow uses examples and structured source files.
- No performance statistic, customer quote, or citation uplift was inferred from this process.
- The draft avoids claims that structured content, schema, or high publishing volume automatically creates AI citations. [Google's helpful-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) and [AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) support a focus on original value and ordinary SEO foundations.
- [GOV.UK's content model guidance](https://design-guide.publishing.service.gov.uk/glossary/content-model/) supports the public explanation of organizing content into reusable types, attributes, and relationships.
- Current Enablement [LinkedIn thought leadership service](https://www.enablement.ch/linkedin-thoughtleadership) and [content-to-meetings article](https://www.enablement.ch/blog/how-to-turn-linkedin-content-into-qualified-sales-meetings) ground the service connection and internal links.
